# 🚦 Eth Transport (Transport DAO)

### *Decentralized Road Safety, Incident Management & Transport Infrastructure Governance*

Transport DAO is an open-source Web3 platform that empowers **citizens**, **police**, and **transport authorities** to collaboratively improve **road safety**, **incident reporting**, and **transport infrastructure management** using **Ethereum, Filecoin, IPFS, Chainlink, Livepeer**, and the broader decentralized stack.

Our mission is to build a **transparent, verifiable, and citizen-participatory transport system** that reduces accidents, improves emergency response, and enhances mobility services through decentralized technology.

---

## ✅ Key Goals

* **Improve road safety and reduce congestion** through real-time incident reporting and video analytics.
* **Crowdsource road intelligence** from citizens, drivers, and civic authorities.
* **Use Filecoin/IPFS for secure, permanent storage** of visual evidence, alarms, analytics, and transport asset data.
* **Introduce Transport DAOs** for transparent decision-making, vehicle service tracking, and maintenance governance.
* **Enable predictive analytics** and verifiable incident history using decentralized data.

---

## 🧩 Core Features

### 🔍 Incident Reporting + Video Intelligence

* Add/manage cameras for incident coverage
* Live video streaming using **Livepeer**
* Object detection & real-time analytics
* Store snapshots + analytics on **IPFS / Filecoin**
* Automatic alarms with on-chain metadata

### 🚨 Alarm & Evidence Management

* Store/delete alarms and incident evidence on IPFS
* Anchor IPFS hashes on Ethereum (testnets)
* View alarms, blockchain TXs, and metadata links
* Mobile-first interface for field officers

### 🗳 Transport DAO & Governance

* NFT-gated proposals & voting (Filecoin FVM)
* Service providers mint RFP NFTs
* Gasless, IPFS-backed voting with verifiable CID chains
* DAO-based contract work management and tender governance

### 🚗 Vehicle & Asset Registry (NFC + NFTs)

* NFT-based identification for vehicles & spare parts
* Anti-counterfeiting for vital components
* Inter-state travel, license info & pollution checks stored on decentralized infra

### 💬 Communication & Alerts

* XMTP messaging for admin regions
* Push Protocol alerts for ICM and weekly dashboards
* Token-gated access for 5 personas (Citizens, Police, Dispatchers, Admins, Govt)

### 📊 Transport Analytics & Dashboards

* Covalent-powered vehicle, NFT, and asset dashboards
* Filecoin Data Tools for asset indexing & retrieval
* EtherCalc-based collaboration spreadsheets
* ZK Transport Wallet for secure cross-chain interactions

## 🧱 Architecture Overview

### Filecoin & IPFS

We use Filecoin and IPFS for:

* storing incident snapshots, video frames, alarm metadata
* decentralized notes & camera configs
* NFT.Storage for incident NFT archival
* FVM smart contracts for:

  * Transport NFTs (vehicle/service provider tags)
  * DAO voting
  * Data DAOs & verifiable audit trails

Example modules:

* **Incident Notes (Tableland + IPFS)**
* **w3ui-based storage for streaming snapshots**
* **Livepeer + IPFS video archiving**
* **Data DAO infrastructure for regional transport**

### Chainlink

* Vehicle API integration (Tesla → Chainlink EA; planning Hyundai/Maruti)
* Chainlink VRF for E-Challan / bill generation
* Parametric insurance for public transport
* Decentralized ambulance routing (Link My Ride extension)

### Livepeer

* Real-time streaming for road incidents
* Snapshot + object detection
* Video NFT creation

### Consensys + Partner Tools

* Infura RPC for alarms + metadata
* Metamask Unity SDK for spare-parts marketplace
* Linea for social sentiment analytics
* Zora for marketplaces (alarm wallpapers, tones)

### Optimism, BTTC, Fluence & More

* OP NFT marketplace for spare parts + work orders
* Fluence counter for citizen-reported incidents
* Tron DAO community coordination modules
